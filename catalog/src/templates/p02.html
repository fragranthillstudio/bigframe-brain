The range at a glance

Eighteen structured-light 3D cameras in five series, with fields of view from 9.8 mm to 3.6 m. One software platform, TridiVision, across the range. Every figure is stated the same way: field of view at the near, optimum and far working distance, point spacing at the optimum, Z repeatability as the regional 2σ value (definitions and method, page 22).
