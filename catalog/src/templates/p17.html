Selecting a model

Choose a field of view that covers the part with margin at the optimum working distance.

Choose a point spacing of one tenth of the smallest feature or finer.

Choose a Z repeatability of one tenth of the tolerance or better; a gauge study on the part decides.

Which model? Ask us.

Send a photograph of the part, the feature and its tolerance to info@bigframe.com. We come back with the model and the working distance, and say what the camera will and will not resolve.
