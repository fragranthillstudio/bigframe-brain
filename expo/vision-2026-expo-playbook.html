Executive summary

VISION 2026 is the first show after three years of contraction in the European machine-vision market, and the first where Chinese 3D vendors arrive in force. Big Frame goes in with five camera series, one debut (PRS-2500B, 1.3 to 3.6 m field of view), and one job: leave with parts in hand and three distributor letters of intent, not a bag of badges.

01

Why this show, why this year

Europe's 3D segment grows faster than the market

Europe 3D machine vision about USD 2.07 bn in 2025, 11.4 % CAGR to 2033, Germany the largest country, UK the fastest. Whole European MV market 7.3 % CAGR. Analysts disagree on absolute size; the direction is consistent. Inference

Chinese vendors arrive together, on price

Hikrobot, Huaray/iRAYPLE (8E20), Daheng, Mech-Mind GmbH, SinceVision (8A30) and several Shenzhen firms are on the exhibitor list. Yole: Chinese firms expand abroad "leveraging a significant price advantage" and are "putting pressure on prices". If Big Frame stands next to them without a technical story, we are read as one more price player. Verified

Nobody's repeatability figure means the same thing

Zivid quotes 1 σ point precision, Photoneo temporal noise plus calibration accuracy, Mech-Mind and LMI Z repeatability σ plus VDI/VDE 2634-2, IDS maximum deviation, SICK ± at 2 m. German buyers know this. The vendor who states the definition on the sheet wins the technical conversation. Verified

Precedent. Zivid entered Germany through partner stands and a STEMMER agreement (2019) before it had a German office; Photoneo used Rauscher for DACH and Multipix for the UK, then showed live demos at VISION 2024 and was bought by Zebra in 2025; Mech-Mind opened Munich in 2019–21 and reached about 20 % overseas revenue by 2022 through integrator partners and an MVTec interface. All three sold through a European distributor first and hired later. Big Frame should follow the same sequence. Where the analogy breaks: those three had application references in Europe before the show. We have none yet, so the show has to produce them (Outcome 2).

Product promotion and the PRS-2500B debut

Five series, one sentence each, and the one claim we never make. The stand tells one story: repeatability you can measure on our stand, from 10 mm to 3.6 m field of view, at a mid-range price.

TRS · 4 models
FOV 9.8–74 mm · Z rep. 0.06–1 µm

Micro-structure inspection where a profiler is too slow: 26 M points per frame.

Do not say "accuracy". Say regional Z repeatability, 1 σ, and name the target.

VRH9 · 7 models
FOV 24–277 mm · Z rep. 0.15–1.8 µm · 1.8 kg

Sub-micron class from 24 mm to 277 mm in one housing family. Against Micro-Epsilon surfaceCONTROL and wenglor MLAS.

Do not compare against Keyence VR-6000 (benchtop profiler, different job).

VR · 4 models
FOV 300–1,500 mm · Z rep. 2–100 µm · 1.6 kg

Bin picking and robot guidance at Zivid 2+ and Mech-Eye PRO working distances, lighter than both.

VR-600B is 14 µm and VR-1300B is 60 µm; the printed manual is wrong. Only the catalog Rev C figures go on the stand.

VRD · 2 models
FOV 580–1,166 mm · Z rep. 6–24 µm · 0.87 kg

Colour stereo for cobots and AMRs, under 900 g. This is the Motek-side product.

Do not position it against ToF (Helios2, blaze): different price tier and different job.

PRS-2500B · debut
FOV 1.3–3.6 m · WD 1.2–3.5 m · Z rep. 300 µm · 3.9 kg

Full pallet and full cage in one frame, RGB registered to the cloud, 638 nm laser. Positioned against Zivid 3 XL250, PhoXi XL and Mech-Eye DEEP / LSR L.

Baseline is "approx. 300 mm" and laser class is "stated at launch". Both go on the sheet as preliminary. Never quote "on request" for points per frame; say "5 MP stereo pair".

Stand discipline that decides the numbers.
  • Nobody sits. Nobody eats on the stand. Phones in hand only at the aisle.
  • Every visitor is asked three questions in the first minute: what part, what plant, what does it cost you today when it fails.
  • A promised follow-up date is a contract. It goes on the lead card in front of the visitor.
  • Competitor stands: take the datasheet, ask the price band, write the repeatability wording. Do not photograph with a phone held up.
  • German is spoken first at German stands; the catalog DE goes to any German-speaking visitor.

Terms we walk in with

Distributor margin 25–35 % off list (industry inference from Photoneo and Mech-Mind reseller pricing, not a quoted figure); demo stock of one VR and one VRD on consignment for six months; application support from Suzhou within 48 hours; territory non-exclusive for year one, exclusive on volume. Assumption to test

The one rule

One DACH partner, not two. Rauscher and MaxxVision both selling us in Germany produces a price war on our own product in year one. Decision 2 picks one; the other gets Austria or Switzerland if they want it.

Direct accounts stay direct

Mech-Mind GmbH (Germering) and Hikrobot Europe (Hoofddorp) sell direct with local staff. We are not there yet: no European service, no spares. Until a service partner exists, every European lead goes through a distributor, including the ones we meet ourselves at the show.

The 60-second talk track

  1. "Big Frame makes structured-light 3D cameras from 10 mm to 3.6 m field of view. Five series, one software."
  2. "We publish repeatability, not accuracy, and we measure it on the stand. Here are this hour's three captures."
  3. "What is the part, which plant, and what does a miss cost you today?"
  4. "We test your part in Suzhou within a week and send a report in this format." Hand over the catalog open at page 17.
  5. Write the lead card with the visitor watching. Agree the follow-up date out loud.
